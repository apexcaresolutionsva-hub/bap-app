Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

You can cancel your BAP membership anytime, new members can get a full refund within 7 days, and unused credit packs are refundable within 30 days of purchase.

Purchase Cancel Refund
Membership, first month Anytime Full refund if you ask within 7 days of your first charge
Membership, later months Anytime; access continues to the end of the paid month No refunds for partial months
Credit pack, nothing used n/a Full refund if you ask within 30 days of purchase
Credit pack, partly used n/a Not refundable; remaining credits stay on your account
A service that failed n/a Not charged: misses, errors and incomplete results are never billed

How to cancel

Open BAP → Services → Manage billing → Cancel membership. You will not be charged again, and member pricing continues until your paid period ends.

How to request a refund

Email support@buybap.com from your account email with the date and amount of the charge. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe, usually within 5–10 business days.

Billing errors

If you were charged twice or charged for a service that did not run, email us within 60 days and we will correct it.

Disputes

Please contact us before filing a chargeback; most issues are fixed within one business day.
