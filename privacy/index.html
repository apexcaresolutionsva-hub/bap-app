Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

BAP (Apex Care Solutions VA LLC, dba BAP) collects only what it needs to run the Service, never sells your account information, lets property owners opt out of BAP's lists, and lets you ask us to see, correct or delete your data. This policy covers buybap.com and app.buybap.com.

2. How we use it

We use information to provide and improve the Service, process payments, prevent fraud and abuse, answer support requests, send account and billing emails, and meet legal obligations. We do not use your data to train third-party AI models.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help run BAP, each limited to what its job requires:

  • Stripe for payments and fraud prevention.
  • Supabase for database hosting and sign-in.
  • Google for email and website analytics.
  • Our skip-tracing provider (the property address only) when you run a skip trace.
  • RentCast (the property address only) when you request a value estimate.
  • Anthropic (the lead's property and deal details) when you run an AI deal analysis.
  • Wix and GitHub for hosting our website and app.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business. We never sell your account information (your name, email, payment details or the work you save in BAP), and we do not share it for targeted advertising. Property and owner information from public records is different: providing it to paying members is BAP's core service, and section 4 explains how owners can opt out.

4. Property owners: how to opt out

BAP compiles property records that include owner names, mailing addresses and, when a member runs a skip trace, owner phone numbers and emails, and makes them available to paying members.

If you own property and want your information removed from or corrected in BAP, email support@buybap.com with the property address. We will suppress your contact information from members' searches and respond within 30 days. Texas residents can use the same address to exercise their rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (section 6).

5. Cookies and analytics

buybap.com uses Google Analytics cookies to understand site traffic. The app uses essential browser storage to keep you signed in. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the app may not work without essential storage.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (including under the Texas and Virginia privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising. Email support@buybap.com to make a request. We will verify your identity, respond within 45 days, and never treat you differently for using these rights. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision.

7. How long we keep data

We keep account and work data while your account is open, and delete it within 90 days after you ask us to close your account, except records we must keep for tax, accounting or legal reasons. Demo sessions are temporary and removed periodically.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, payments are handled by Stripe, and access to each account's data is restricted to that account. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Children

BAP is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

We will post updates here and email you about material changes before they take effect.
